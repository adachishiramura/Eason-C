"When Eason sings, it feels like he is conversing directly with your soul."

Born in Hong Kong in 1974, Eason Chan Yick-shun studied architecture and vocal music at Kingston University and the Royal Academy of Music in London, earning his Grade 8 Vocal certification. In 1995, after returning to Hong Kong, he won the prestigious 14th New Talent Singing Awards, sparking an unprecedented career.

Chan's voice is celebrated for its warm baritone depth, effortless falsetto transitions, and uninhibited emotional vulnerability. In 2005, his album U87 was named by Time Magazine as one of the 5 Asian albums worth buying.

3x Golden Melody Award

Global Stage Record
